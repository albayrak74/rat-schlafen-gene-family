The rat genome has eight annotated Schlafen genes, seven in a chromosome 10 cluster
Rat Slfn9 matches mouse Slfn8/9; rat and mouse Slfn3 and Slfn4 are co-orthologues
Schlafen promoters are enriched for interferon-stimulated response elements
Higher Schlafen expression in aged rat organs partly tracks leukocyte marker levels
Slfn4 is higher in inflamed colitis mucosa but not after LPS-induced lung injury
