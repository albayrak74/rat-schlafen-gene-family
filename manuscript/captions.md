Fig. 1 Organization of the Schlafen gene clusters on rat chromosome 10 (GRCr8), mouse chromosome 11 (GRCm39) and human chromosome 17 (GRCh38). Arrows show gene position and orientation, drawn to scale within each cluster. Colors mark the length-based groups (group I, Slfn1/2 and SLFN12/12L; group II, Slfn3/4; group III, the long proteins), and gray lines connect rat and mouse genes at corresponding positions; rat and mouse Slfn3 and Slfn4 are co-orthologues rather than one-to-one orthologues (Fig. 2a). Slfnl1 lies outside the cluster in each species (rat chromosome 5, mouse chromosome 4, human chromosome 1)

Fig. 2 Phylogeny and domain architecture of the Schlafen proteins. (a) Maximum-likelihood tree of 24 Schlafen proteins from rat (red), mouse and human, inferred with IQ-TREE 2 under the JTT+F+G4 model from a MAFFT alignment trimmed to 614 positions and rooted on human SLFNL1. Numbers at nodes are ultrafast bootstrap values (1000 replicates); values below 70 are not shown. (b) Pfam domains of the eight rat proteins detected with gathering thresholds: SLFN N-terminal domain (B3R, PF17057), AlbA_2 core (PF04326), SLFN GTPase-like domain (PF21026) and SLFN group 3 helicase domain (PF09848). The hatched box marks the helicase-like region of Slfn14 detected only with a profile built from mammalian Schlafen sequences. Protein length is given at the right

Fig. 3 Gene structure, promoter motifs and selective constraint of the rat Schlafen genes. (a) Exon–intron structure of the representative transcripts (Table 2), drawn 5′ to 3′; blue boxes are coding sequence and gray boxes untranslated regions. (b) Numbers of ISRE, GAS, IRF-E and NF-κB consensus motifs on both strands of the 2 kb upstream of the transcription start site of each representative transcript; the mean count in 200 random 2-kb windows of chromosome 10 is given below. (c) Ka/Ks ratios of rat–mouse gene pairs at corresponding cluster positions (blue) and of the rat paralogue pairs with unsaturated synonymous sites (orange), estimated with the Nei–Gojobori method; the dashed line marks Ka/Ks = 1

Fig. 4 Expression of Schlafen genes across organs and ages in the Fischer 344 rat BodyMap (GSE53960; 320 samples). (a) Mean log2(CPM + 1) in eleven organs, averaged over sexes and ages; τ is the tissue-specificity index, which is not informative for Slfn3 and Slfn14 because they were barely detected. (b) Mean expression of Slfn2, Slfn4, Slfn5 and Slfn9 over all organs at 2, 6, 21 and 104 weeks of age. (c–e) Expression of Slfn4 (c), Slfn9 (d) and Slfnl1 (e) by age in each organ; colored lines mark the organs with the largest changes and gray lines the other organs

Fig. 5 Age and sex differences in Schlafen expression in the rat BodyMap. (a) log2 fold changes between 104-week-old and 21-week-old rats in eleven organs, adjusted for sex where both sexes were available. (b) log2 fold changes between female and male rats in nine organs, adjusted for age. Asterisks mark the Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across all gene–organ tests of each comparison: * < 0.05, ** < 0.01, *** < 0.001. n.e., not expressed (not tested). The liver Slfnl1 value has no P value because it was withheld by the DESeq2 outlier filter. Slfn3 and Slfn14 are omitted because they were not expressed in any organ

Fig. 6 Schlafen expression in Wistar rat models of inflammation, obesity and liver aging. Cells show log2 fold changes. Blocks from top to bottom: TNBS colitis (GSE149517), acute inflammation models (LPS lung injury, GSE228906; adjuvant arthritis, liver, GSE261056; IL-1β-exposed microglia, GSE298271), diet-induced obesity (liver, GSE192425) and liver aging and sex (GSE160153). Asterisks mark FDR across the Schlafen genes within each comparison: * < 0.05, ** < 0.01, *** < 0.001. n.d., not detected or not annotated in the dataset

Fig. 7 Cross-validated prediction of age and inflammation from Schlafen expression. (a) AUC for aged (104 weeks) versus adult (21 weeks) samples in each held-out organ, for elastic-net models built on Slfn2, Slfn4, Slfn5 and Slfn9, on four canonical ISGs and on the 2,000 genes with the highest within-organ variance; pooled AUC values are given in the legend. (b) Pooled AUC of the Schlafen model (orange line) compared with 200 random four-gene sets (gray histogram). (c) Mean absolute SHAP values of the four Schlafen genes in a random-forest age model; values are in-sample; orange and blue bars mark genes whose higher expression is associated with predictions of the aged and adult class, respectively, and the gray bar a gene without a consistent direction. (d) AUC for inflamed versus non-inflamed samples in each held-out dataset (uninflamed colon segments of TNBS-treated rats grouped with controls), for the Schlafen genes, four ISGs and the 2,000 genes with the highest within-dataset variance, in leave-one-dataset-out validation; the dashed line marks chance level
